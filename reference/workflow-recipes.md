# Workflow recipes

These recipes are selection aids, not mandatory decks. Remove any slide that does not serve the audience or evidence.

## Conference / 学会口演

Confirm meeting, audience, time, language, submission rules, title, authorship, affiliation, COI, funding, ethics, registration, and citations. Build a claim-led sequence: context, question, methods, verified results, limitations, conclusion. Keep every statistical visual tied to structured source data.

## Journal club / 抄読会

Require the paper or verified citation. Cover question, PICO, design, flow, baseline, effect estimates, risk of bias, certainty/applicability, and discussion. Distinguish the authors' claims from the presenter's appraisal.

## Case conference / 症例カンファレンス

Stop for possible PHI. Use relative dates and the minimum necessary clinical detail. Build case framing, timeline, findings, differential evidence, decision point, outcome, and learning points. Do not turn the example into treatment advice.

## Teaching lecture / 教育講演

Define learners and objectives. Alternate short explanations with a choice component, reveal, comparison, or diagram. Separate recall questions from clinical decisions. Cite every externally checkable clinical claim.

## Research progress / 研究進捗

Show the research question, current dataset state, validated flow counts, analysis plan, completed results, unresolved quality issues, and next decisions. Mark interim, exploratory, and unverified outputs explicitly.

## Guideline summary / ガイドライン解説

Require title, issuing body, version/date, region, population, setting, access date, and original source. Separate recommendation strength, evidence certainty, exceptions, and local applicability. Never reconstruct an algorithm from memory.
