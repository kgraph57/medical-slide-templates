How it works

From request to the podium. One flow, fully offline.

Describe

Hand over the paper, abstract, notes, table, or CSV and say what the talk is. The Skill infers presentation type, language, audience, and duration.

Pass the safety gate

PHI, citations, statistics, image rights, COI, and ethics fields are checked. Anything unverifiable stays marked SOURCE NOT PROVIDED and blocks final status.

Present

Present in a browser from the bundled localhost server, or print to a one-slide-per-page PDF. No internet required at any point.

Safety gate

The Skill stops instead of completing missing medical facts.

This project creates clinician-facing academic and teaching presentations. It is not patient material, diagnosis, treatment selection, or clinical decision support.

GateBehavior
PHIStops and asks for corrected or de-identified input when patient-identifying information may be present, including DICOM metadata and burned-in image identifiers.
CitationsBlocks final status when a citation, DOI, PMID, or guideline version cannot be verified. Never invents a reference.
NumbersFlags conflicts in denominators, percentages, units, confidence intervals, p values, and flow totals before preview.
COI / ethicsNever defaults COI to none or ethics to approved. Unanswered required fields stay unconfirmed / SOURCE NOT PROVIDED.
ImagesAsks for provenance, license, consent, or permission when image rights are unknown.
Synthetic contentEvery catalog and example slide carries a permanent visible marker and must never be reused as evidence.

Author

Built by a practicing pediatrician who got tired of 2 a.m. slide work.

Medical Slide started as a personal tool for conference talks and journal clubs: the presentations clinicians actually give, under the time pressure they actually have.

The safety gate exists because medical content generated by a confident system is a real risk. This project chooses to stop, mark, and ask rather than complete missing facts.

Ken Okamoto is a pediatrician and AI healthcare entrepreneur. He writes, teaches, and builds tools at the intersection of clinical practice and applied AI.
